# Theme backgrounds

The Geometric Rhapsody and Blood Moon Castle images were generated with ChatGPT's built-in image-generation tool for this project. The generated originals were 1643×957 pixels; the website exports are resized to exactly **1200×700** with Lanczos resampling and encoded as WebP at quality 94. Star Voyage uses the background supplied on 2026-10-07, already 1200×700, converted to WebP at quality 94 without cropping or changing its composition. No interface elements are baked into the images.

## Generation prompts

### Geometric Rhapsody

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a finished panoramic background artwork for the Geometric Rhapsody playing-card theme, exactly 1200 pixels wide by 700 pixels high (12:7 landscape).
Primary request: an elegant richly textured abstract geometric painting in the same visual language as a deck of abstract geometric playing cards.
Style: sophisticated Bauhaus and constructivist abstraction, tactile aged ivory paper, subtle gouache and lithographic grain, ink-black and midnight-navy geometric forms, cobalt and turquoise accents, ochre gold and vermilion red.
Composition: full-bleed panoramic artwork of rhythmically interlocking large circles, crescents, semicircles, triangles and diagonal bars, with delicate black and brass linework and sparse small dots. Confident balanced visual rhythm, varied scale, expressive but orderly. Large calm cream areas between the shapes; main forms remain readable at small size. Lower central region a little quieter for game elements placed later by the website. This is an abstract painting, not a poker table or a UI mockup.
Constraints: no words, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, playing cards, suit symbols, figures, decorative frame or border. Opaque full-bleed image. Output dimensions 1200x700.
```

### Blood Moon Castle

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: a finished cinematic panoramic background artwork for the Blood Moon Castle playing-card theme, exactly 1200 pixels wide by 700 pixels high (12:7 landscape).
Primary request: a Gothic vampire castle on the night of a blood moon, in a hyperrealistic photographic style.
Scene: an imposing ancient dark-stone Gothic castle on a rocky ridge, with elegant pointed towers, spires, Gothic tracery, flying buttresses, weathered masonry, and a few dimly glowing red amber windows. A huge deep crimson blood moon hangs above the castle through thin moving cloud. Mist drifts through the valley and around the stone foundations. Deep blue slate night sky with natural atmospheric depth.
Composition: wide establishing view, the castle is clearly recognizable and centered, its rich architectural silhouette and red moon visually commanding. Enough open night sky and misty terrain around it for a game interface to later overlay. Place the architecture in the middle band and make lower foreground quieter. No characters.
Light and material: plausible cinematic night photography, soft crimson lunar backlight on spire edges, cool ambient light revealing sharply detailed stone, subtle wet-stone glints and volumetric fog. Moody and ominous yet legible, with real stone texture and photographic dynamic range; do not bury the castle in black shadows. High-end film still, not an illustration or cartoon.
Constraints: no text, logos, watermark, frame, playing cards, tabletop, interface, people or gore. Opaque full-bleed image. Output dimensions 1200x700.
```
